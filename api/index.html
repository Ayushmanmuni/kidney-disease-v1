🩺 Where to Get These Medical Values

These clinical parameters are obtained from routine medical tests. Here’s where to find each one:

🩸
Blood Test (CBC + Biochemistry)

Hemoglobin, PCV, WBC, RBC Count, Blood Glucose, Urea, Creatinine, Sodium, Potassium

🧪
Urine Test (Urinalysis)

Specific Gravity, Albumin, Sugar, RBC in Urine, Pus Cells, Pus Cell Clumps, Bacteria

🩺
Blood Pressure Monitor

Diastolic blood pressure — available at any pharmacy, clinic, or home BP device

📝
Medical History (Self-Report)

Hypertension, Diabetes, Heart Disease, Appetite, Swelling, Anemia — from your doctor’s past diagnosis

💡 Tip: You can ask your doctor for your latest blood test report or request a "Renal Profile" / "Kidney Function Test (KFT)" which covers most of these parameters.
